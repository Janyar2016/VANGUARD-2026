# AI Assistance Disclosure

**Project:** ASTRA HEALTH  
**Team:** VANGUARD POLARIS  
**Challenge:** NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

The team developed the project concept, selected its scope and features, made the final design decisions, and is responsible for reviewing, testing, and presenting the submitted work.

AI assistance, including ChatGPT, was used as a support tool during parts of the development process. This assistance included explaining programming concepts, suggesting and revising code, helping identify and correct software issues, improving documentation, and assisting with wording and organization.

The team reviewed the resulting code and written materials, ran the application locally, and remains responsible for the accuracy and final content of the submission. AI assistance should not be interpreted as independent scientific validation, clinical review, or endorsement by NASA or the Canadian Space Agency.

The current ASTRA HEALTH monitoring engine is a transparent, rule-based demonstration system. It is not a trained AI or machine-learning model.

This disclosure describes the assistance used in preparing the project and should be checked by all team members for completeness before submission.
