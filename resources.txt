# ASTRA HEALTH — External Resources

This file lists the official scientific references and data sources used or referenced by ASTRA HEALTH. The NASA OSDR OSD-575 processed dataset is retrieved by the separate NASA OSDR Research Data page; the other listed NASA and CSA pages are background references.

- Risks of Human Spaceflight: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/

## Important note
No ASTRA HEALTH rule threshold is claimed to be an official NASA/CSA limit or clinically validated range. Consult each source's own terms before reusing content or data. NASA does not endorse non-U.S. Government entities and is not responsible for information on non-U.S. Government websites.
