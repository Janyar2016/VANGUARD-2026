# Problem Statement
## Health Information Challenges During Space Missions

### 1. The Problem

Astronauts experience health and performance challenges in the space environment. Relevant factors include microgravity, radiation exposure, isolation and confinement, restricted living conditions, distance from Earth, and changes in daily routines.

These conditions make it important to collect and review health-related information in a clear and organized way.

### 2. Major Spaceflight Contexts

**Gravity fields:** Long-duration exposure to microgravity and transitions between gravity environments can affect multiple body systems.

**Sleep and daily schedules:** Artificial lighting, demanding schedules, and environmental conditions can affect sleep patterns and daily routines.

**Radiation:** Space radiation is an important health concern, particularly for missions beyond Earth's protective environment.

**Isolation and confinement:** Living and working in a remote, confined environment for extended periods can affect well-being and team dynamics.

**Distance from Earth:** Communication delays and limited access to supplies or medical support can make coordination more challenging.

### 3. The Information-Management Challenge

Health-related information may include physiological measurements, sleep records, environmental context, and astronaut self-reports. When information is recorded at different times or reviewed separately, it may be harder to understand its context or examine changes across available records.

A software tool can help organize these inputs, display historical information, and provide a structured way to record reported symptoms.

### 4. The Need Addressed by ASTRA HEALTH

ASTRA HEALTH explores a local software approach to this information-management challenge. Its prototype features include:
- Organizing manually entered health indicators in one interface.
- Supporting symptom reporting through an interactive body map.
- Displaying trends from available historical records.
- Showing explainable findings from illustrative rule-based checks.
- Creating structured symptom reports and local review-queue items.
- Exporting a JSON snapshot of the available project data.

### 5. Problem Definition

**How can astronaut-health-related information and self-reported symptoms be organized, reviewed, and represented clearly in a software interface designed around the challenges of space missions?**

ASTRA HEALTH explores this question through manual/synthetic data entry, historical visualization, transparent demonstration rules, and a local symptom-review workflow.

The current prototype does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, clinical triage, or communication with an operational medical team.
